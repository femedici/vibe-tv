Vibe TV adapts the quiet top-right utilities, shared rounded dock, restrained
focus plate and matte surface treatment from app/style.css. Its responsive
layout keeps the Vibe TV last-successful-app card, five existing themes and
native application controller. No CleanFrame device helpers, private fonts,
wallpapers, catalogues or Art Mode functionality are included.
